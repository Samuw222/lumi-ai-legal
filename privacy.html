Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Lumi AI is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Lumi AI mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials
  • Google account information (if you choose Google Sign-In)

Financial Information

To provide budgeting, expense tracking, backup, and synchronization features, we may collect and store:

  • Expense records
  • Income records
  • Financial goals
  • Financial preferences and settings

AI Features

When you use Lumi AI features, we may process:

  • Messages sent to Lumi
  • Financial questions and requests
  • Voice transcriptions
  • Information extracted from receipts and invoices

Subscription Information

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not collect or store payment card information.

Advertising

The free version of Lumi AI displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (advertising ID) and related technical information. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region and your consent choices.

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we request consent before serving personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the app settings ("Privacidad de anuncios").

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Lumi Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Technical Information

We may automatically collect technical information such as:

  • Device information
  • Application version
  • Operating system version
  • Error logs and diagnostics

How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the service
  • Authenticate users
  • Enable cloud synchronization
  • Generate AI-powered financial insights
  • Process subscriptions and premium features
  • Improve security, stability, and performance

Cloud Synchronization

Lumi AI may securely synchronize your financial data between your devices and our servers to provide backup and multi-device access.

Data Storage and Security

Your information may be stored locally on your device and on secure cloud infrastructure operated by us or trusted service providers. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections where available.

Third-Party Services

Lumi AI may use third-party services including:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing
  • RevenueCat
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • AI processing providers

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect users and the platform.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Account deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the app: go to Settings → Eliminar cuenta. This immediately removes all your data from our servers. Alternatively, see our account deletion page or contact us by email.

Children's Privacy

Lumi AI is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page.
